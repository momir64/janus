Privezak — Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

Privezak is a passkey manager for Android. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything it stores stays on your device.

Data we collect

None. Privezak has no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. The app does not hold the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your information anywhere, to us or to anyone else.

What the app stores on your device

  • Passkeys — the credentials you create for websites and apps, together with the site name and username each one belongs to. They are encrypted with AES-GCM using a key derived from your PIN and kept in Privezak's private storage, which other apps cannot read.
  • Your PIN — never stored as typed. It is used to derive the key that unlocks your passkeys.
  • Biometric unlock key — if you enable biometric unlock, the key is held by the Android Keystore and protected by your device's hardware. Your fingerprint or face data itself belongs to Android and is never made available to Privezak.
  • Preferences — small settings such as your chosen session timeout.

Camera

Privezak asks for camera access only so you can scan a sign-in QR code. Frames are analysed on the device as you point the camera and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted. You can decline the permission and continue to use every other part of the app.

Backups and transfers

Privezak opts out of Android's automatic cloud backup and of device-to-device transfer, so your passkeys are not copied to Google Drive or moved to a new phone. They exist only on the device where you created them.

Sharing

Nothing is shared, because nothing leaves your device. When you sign in to a site, Android's credential manager hands that site only the passkey signature it needs — the same exchange any passkey provider performs — and no other information passes through us.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual passkeys inside the app at any time. Uninstalling Privezak removes all of its stored data from the device permanently. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no request you need to send us.

Children

Privezak is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes
